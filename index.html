Questions, answered

Clarity
before connection.

Is ConnectIQ another MES?+

The focus is connected factory context. USAL and the Factory State Engine sit beneath Manufacturing Memory and applications such as FDM. Integrations can complement existing ERP or MES workflows rather than requiring a full replacement.

Can every reader be flashed in a browser?+

No. Hardware compatibility depends on the approved profile, bootloader and communication protocol. Browser-based flashing requires a validated device-specific adapter and a supported browser.

Does payment mean a device is already activated?+

No. Payment or an approved credit arrangement establishes eligibility. Device verification, compatible firmware provisioning and backend-confirmed attestation are separate steps before activation is complete.

What happens to factory data and IP?+

Access should be limited to authorised users and approved integrations. Customer data rights, retention and any use of de-identified data must be specified in the service agreement. Source code and signing keys are not part of a device activation purchase.
