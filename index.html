Trust and governance

Built for factories that cannot risk a black box.

Clear security and ownership boundaries for OEMs, system integrators and plant IT teams.

Does an OEM receive ConnectIQ source code?

No. Approved OEMs receive a controlled flashing package or factory tool that provisions signed images to registered devices. Signing keys, activation services and the event-memory layer remain under ConnectIQ control.

What happens after payment?

The system creates a short-lived provisioning request tied to the customer, device identity, firmware channel and purchased capabilities. The request is consumed during flashing and recorded in an immutable audit trail.

Can a customer use the hardware without subscribing?

Yes, in a basic state: the device remains ConnectIQ-ready and can operate with limited local functions. Manufacturing-memory, workflow and AI capabilities activate only when the relevant entitlement is present.

Who owns factory data?

You own your operational data. ConnectIQ uses it only to provide the service and maintain security, and—where contractually agreed—uses de-identified patterns to improve connectors and models.
